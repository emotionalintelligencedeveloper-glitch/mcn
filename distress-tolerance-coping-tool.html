Distress Tolerance Coping Tool

Distress tolerance skills help you get through intense emotional moments without making things worse. This tool is for moments when feelings are high and calm feels out of reach.

1) What feels most overwhelming right now?

2) What sensations are you noticing in your body?

3) What thoughts are looping or repeating?

4) What urge do you feel when distress peaks?

5) What has helped even slightly in past difficult moments?
