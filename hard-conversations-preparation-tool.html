Hard Conversations — Preparation Tool

This tool helps you slow down, organise your thoughts, and prepare for a difficult conversation with clarity, calm, and respect — whether it’s with a partner, family member, colleague, or friend.

This is a preparation space.
You don’t need perfect words. You just need honesty, grounding, and intention.

1) What conversation are you preparing for?

2) What emotions are coming up for you right now?

3) What is the core message or boundary you want to express?

4) What outcome would feel healthy — even if it’s not perfect?

5) What might the other person feel or react with?

6) How can you stay grounded if the conversation feels tense?
