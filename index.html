Emotional Intelligence Foundations

This course is about learning how to relate to your emotions with clarity, steadiness, and self-trust — even when life feels messy.

You will learn how to:
  • Recognise emotions before they overwhelm you
  • Name what you feel without judgement
  • Pause instead of reacting on autopilot
  • Understand emotional triggers with compassion
  • Build emotional confidence over time

Lesson 1: Emotional Awareness

Awareness is the foundation of emotional intelligence. If an emotion is ignored, it often grows louder.

Practice:
Pause for 10 seconds. Scan your body. Where do you feel tension, heaviness, or ease?

Lesson 2: Naming Emotions

Naming emotions creates clarity. Clarity reduces emotional overload.

Practice:
Instead of “I’m fine,” try: “I feel ___ because ___.”

Lesson 3: The Power of the Pause

A pause interrupts emotional autopilot. It gives your nervous system room to settle.

Practice:
Breathe in for 4, out for 6. Repeat twice.

Lesson 4: Emotional Triggers

Triggers are learned responses. Understanding them replaces shame with insight.

Lesson 5: Emotional Confidence

Emotional confidence is trusting yourself to stay present — even when emotions are uncomfortable.
