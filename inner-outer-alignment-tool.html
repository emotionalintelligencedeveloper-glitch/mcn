Inner vs Outer Self Reflection Tool

This gentle tool helps you explore the difference between how you feel inside and how you show up in the world. There are no right answers — only honesty.

1) How do you truly feel inside most days?

2) How do you usually present yourself to others?

3) Where do you notice a gap between your inner and outer self?

4) What do you hide, soften, or adjust about yourself?

5) What might change if your inner and outer self felt more aligned?
