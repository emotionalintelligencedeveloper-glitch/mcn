Why people-pleasing can create anxiety

When you keep saying yes to stay safe, avoid conflict, or keep people happy, your nervous system learns: “If I don’t manage others, something bad will happen.” Over time, that can fuel anxiety, resentment, and even avoiding going out — because you expect people will take advantage.
