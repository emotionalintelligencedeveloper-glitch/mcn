AI Bubble Fear (2026) — A Grounded Check-In

News about AI, automation, layoffs, and “bubbles” can quietly activate fear. This tool helps you slow down, separate fear from facts, and reconnect with what you can realistically control.

Important: If anxiety feels overwhelming or begins to affect your safety, please reach out to a trusted person or professional. This tool supports reflection — not prediction or financial advice.

1) What worries you most when you hear about AI and the future?

2) How intense does this fear feel right now?

3) Which area of life feels most affected?

4) How much control do you feel you have right now?

5) What would feeling “prepared” actually look like for you?
