Emotional Intelligence Foundations

A free, self-paced course to help you understand your emotions, pause before reacting, and respond to everyday life with more clarity.


Lesson 1: Noticing What You Feel

Emotional intelligence begins with awareness, not change.

Practice: Pause once today and silently name one emotion.

What you gain:
  • Emotional awareness
  • Less automatic reaction
  • Inner steadiness

Lesson 2: Naming Emotions Without Judgement

Language creates clarity and reduces emotional intensity.

Practice: Say “I notice I feel…”

What you gain:
  • Better emotional vocabulary
  • Reduced overwhelm
  • Self-understanding

Lesson 3: Pausing Before You React

A pause creates choice.

Practice: Take one slow breath before responding.

What you gain:
  • Improved communication
  • Fewer regretful reactions
  • Greater control

Lesson 4: Understanding Emotional Triggers

Triggers are patterns, not flaws.

Practice: Notice one trigger without analysing it.

What you gain:
  • Reduced self-blame
  • Emotional insight
  • Greater resilience

Lesson 5: Building Emotional Confidence

Confidence comes from self-trust.

Practice: Speak to yourself kindly today.

What you gain:
  • Self-trust
  • Reduced self-criticism
  • Emotional balance

Lesson 6: Carrying This Into Everyday Life

Emotional intelligence is ongoing.

Practice: Choose one practice to return to.

What you gain:
  • Sustainable growth
  • Long-term clarity
  • Emotional steadiness
