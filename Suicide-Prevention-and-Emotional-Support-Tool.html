You Matter

If you are here, something feels heavy. This space is for the next few minutes only — no pressure, no judgment. You are not alone in this moment.

Wanting the pain to stop is not the same as wanting life to end. What you are feeling right now is a signal — not a verdict on your worth or your future.
If you are in immediate danger: Contact local emergency services now, or ask someone nearby to help you call.

Before anything else — one 30-second pause

  • Breathe in slowly through your nose for 4 counts
  • Hold gently for 3 counts
  • Breathe out slowly through your mouth for 6 counts
  • Repeat once more if you can

Strong urges rise and fall like waves. Waiting — even 10 minutes — can make a difference. You do not have to decide anything right now.
