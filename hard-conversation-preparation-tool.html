Step 5 — Grounding before you speak

Take 30 or 60 seconds to breathe and settle before the conversation. Optional but effective.

00:00
Press a button below to begin. Inhale gently… exhale slowly.
Breathing guide: Inhale for 4 counts… hold for 2… exhale slowly for 6. Let your shoulders drop. Let your jaw soften. You are preparing — not performing.

Step 7 — Practice mode

Read your draft out loud. Edit it here until it sounds like you. This builds real confidence.

Tips: Try reading it once out loud. Then try starting with one warm sentence — "I care about this and I want to speak honestly." Notice how your body feels when you say it.
