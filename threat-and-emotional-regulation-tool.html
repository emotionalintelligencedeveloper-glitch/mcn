Threat & Emotional Regulation Tool

If you feel close to snapping, overwhelmed by anger, fear, or adrenaline, this tool helps you slow the reaction before it controls you.

If you feel at risk of harming yourself or someone else: please step away from others and contact emergency services or a trusted person immediately.

1) What situation or thought is making you feel on edge right now?

2) What emotion feels strongest?

3) How does your body feel right now?

4) What could happen if this reaction takes over?

5) What would help you feel even 10% calmer?
