Courage in the Face of Fear

Fear is a natural human response — it appears when something matters. Courage does not mean fear disappears. It means learning how to move forward with awareness, self-respect, and care.

This short reflection will help you:

  • Understand what your fear is really about
  • Notice how it influences your choices
  • Reconnect with moments where you were already brave
  • Choose one realistic next step
There are no right answers here — only honest ones.

1) What fear has been showing up most strongly for you lately?

Be specific. Name the fear without judging it.

2) How does this fear affect your choices, behaviour, or confidence?

Notice patterns: avoidance, overthinking, hesitation, or self-doubt.

3) What do you believe this fear is trying to protect you from?

Fear often aims to prevent pain, embarrassment, or loss — even when it overreacts.

4) When have you acted with courage before, even while afraid?

Courage can be quiet: speaking up once, asking for help, or not giving up.

5) What is one small, realistic act of courage you could take next?

If it feels overwhelming, make it smaller.
