Inner vs Outer Self — Reflection Tool

This reflection helps you explore the space between who you are inside and how you show up to the world — with honesty and care.

This tool is free. There’s no right or wrong way to answer, and nothing is saved. Take your time — even one sentence is enough.

How to use this reflection

Answer honestly for yourself — not how you think you should feel. You don’t need to explain or justify anything.

Progress: 0 / 5 answered Start anywhere

1) How do you feel on the inside, when no one is watching?

Name emotions, body sensations, or inner dialogue.

3
Starter idea: “Lately, underneath everything, I’ve felt…”

2) How do you usually present yourself to others?

Describe your social style, roles, or masks.

3
Starter idea: “Most people see me as someone who…”

3) Where do you notice the biggest gap between inner and outer you?

Think about work, relationships, family, or online spaces.

3
Starter idea: “The biggest mismatch shows up when…”

4) What parts of yourself do you adjust, soften, or hide?

List traits, needs, boundaries, or opinions.

3
Starter idea: “I tend to hide my ____ because…”

5) What do you think alignment might bring into your life?

Focus on what you would gain, not what you’d lose.

3
Starter idea: “If I were more aligned, I would feel…”

Why the PDF helps you
  • Track growth over time: Compare reflections weekly or monthly and notice emotional patterns.
  • Prepare for therapy or coaching: Bring your reflection into sessions so support starts at the real issue faster.
  • Support honest conversations: Clarify what you want to express before talking with someone important to you.
  • Stay private and organized: Keep your reflections offline in one personal folder for future review.
