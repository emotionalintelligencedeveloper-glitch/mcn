          Side page

          Ride the wave: a 10 minute pause

          An urge is like a wave. It rises, peaks and passes, usually within minutes. You do not have to fight it. You only have to wait and watch.

          1. Notice it. Say to yourself, "This is an urge. It is a wave."
          2. Breathe in for 4, out for 6. Let your shoulders drop.
          3. Name where you feel it in your body, and what you need right now.
          4. Keep your hands busy: hold something cool, sip water, or walk around.
