Grief After Violence — A Gentle Support Tool

Grief after violence can feel confusing, heavy, and isolating. This space is here to help you slow down, reflect, and feel less alone.

If you are in immediate danger or feel unsafe: please contact local emergency services or a trusted person right now.

1) What loss or experience has been affecting you most?

2) What emotions keep returning lately?

3) How has this experience changed you or your sense of safety?

4) What helps you feel even slightly grounded?

5) What kind of support do you wish you had right now?
