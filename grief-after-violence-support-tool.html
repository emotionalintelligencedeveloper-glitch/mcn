Grief After Violence Support Tool

A calm reflection space for moments when grief feels heavy, confusing, or lonely. Move at your own pace. Everything here is optional.

If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services now. If you are having thoughts of ending your life or hurting yourself, please reach out to a crisis line or someone near you right away. You can find one for your country at findahelpline.com.

Safer mode is on

Because your device may be watched, the writing sections are hidden and nothing here can be saved. If you can, use a device the other person cannot reach, such as a library computer or a trusted friend's phone. The helplines and the grounding pause are still below.

A 45-second grounding pause

If it feels right, place both feet on the floor, soften your jaw, and breathe a little slower. This is entirely optional.

Press start when you are ready.
Breathing guide: Breathe in gently for 4 counts, hold for 2, then breathe out slowly for 6. Notice one thing you can see and one thing you can feel.

Actions

Continue later

This tool does not require any identifying details and does not send your answers anywhere. If you add your first name for the PDF, it is used only on the PDF and is never saved. If save is on, your draft is stored only in this browser. If you are using a shared or public device, leave save off. The PDF you download will contain what you wrote, so keep it somewhere private.

Get help now

If you need urgent help or local support, these resources are free and available in most countries. If you are in immediate danger, contact emergency services first.

  • Anywhere in the world: findahelpline.com
  • South Africa, grief and loss: findahelpline.com
  • South Africa, trauma: findahelpline.com

If the violence was in a home or relationship

  • South Africa, GBV Command Centre (free, 24 hours): 0800 428 428
  • South Africa, other ways to reach the same centre: SMS HELP to 31531, or send a Please Call Me to 1207867#.
  • Zimbabwe, national GBV call centre (toll free): 575
  • United Kingdom, National Domestic Abuse Helpline (free, 24 hours): 0808 2000 247
  • United Kingdom: live chat and a British Sign Language video service are at nationaldahelpline.org.uk. In an emergency call 999.
  • United States, National Domestic Violence Hotline: 1-800-799-7233
  • United States: you can also text START to 88788, or use the live chat at thehotline.org.
