AI, Work and Life Fear Tool

AI is changing jobs, money, homes and family life. This is a calm space to sort worry from facts, see what is in your control, and take one grounded step this week.

Reflection and wellbeing support only. Not a diagnosis, medical tool or financial advice.

What you feel about AI and the future is real and shared by many people. You are not alone in this.
