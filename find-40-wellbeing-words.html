How to play
  1. Press on the first letter of a word and drag in a straight line. Words go forward, backward, up, down and diagonally.
  2. When you find a word, confetti flies and a card tells you what the word means.
  3. Tap Next word and keep going until you have found all 40.
  4. Stuck? Tap Hint and the first letter of a word will glow.
  5. Add your name, download your PDF with all the meanings and a 7 day plan, and share the game.
  6. Then pick the next round. There are four, each with 40 new words.
See what you get

When you play this game, you build your wellbeing one word at a time. Every word you find comes with a simple meaning you can take into your day.

At the end you get a PDF with your name, all 40 words, what they mean, and a 7 day plan to use them in real life. Tap the button below to see a sample.

  • Share this game with your family and friends so they can play it too.
  • Play this game at school. It is good for all ages.
  • Playing this game is a better way to train your brain than watching videos all the time.

This game is made for desktop and cellphone. It is free with no paywall, because EID focuses on mental wellbeing.
