Courage in the Face of Fear

Fear is a natural response when something meaningful is at stake. This guided reflection helps you slow down, feel supported, and take one courageous next step.

How this journey works

  • Move through 5 short prompts at your own pace.
  • Each step includes gentle guidance.
  • You’ll finish with a personalized courage reflection.

Choose your focus (optional)

Pick the area where fear is showing up most, so the guidance feels more relevant.

Step 1 of 5 20% complete

1) What fear has been showing up most strongly for you lately?

Be specific. Name the fear without judging it.

2) How does this fear affect your choices, behaviour, or confidence?

Notice patterns: avoidance, overthinking, hesitation, or self-doubt.

3) What do you believe this fear is trying to protect you from?

Fear often tries to prevent pain, rejection, embarrassment, or loss.

4) When have you acted with courage before, even while afraid?

Courage can be quiet: asking for help, speaking up once, or not giving up.

5) What is one small, realistic act of courage you could take next?

If it feels overwhelming, make it smaller. Small steps still count.
