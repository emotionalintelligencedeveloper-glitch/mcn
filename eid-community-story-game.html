How it works

  1. Headline Round: submit headline ideas and vote. First headline to 10 votes becomes the chosen headline.
  2. Story Build Round: submit candidate snippets (max 210 chars). First snippet to 10 votes wins the round and is appended.
  3. One winner each round: all other candidates in that round are marked “Not selected this round.”
  4. Conclusion gate: once 396+ words are reached, submit conclusion candidates and vote to lock the ending.
  5. Share to get votes: every headline, snippet, and conclusion candidate has a share button.
