When self help is not enough
Tools like this are great for everyday stress. Please talk to a doctor, counsellor or someone you trust if:
  • Low mood, worry or exhaustion lasts more than two weeks
  • It is affecting your sleep, eating, work or relationships
  • You are leaning on alcohol or other substances to cope
  • You have thoughts of harming yourself
