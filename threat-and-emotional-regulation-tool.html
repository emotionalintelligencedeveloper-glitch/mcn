✨ Step 3 · Regulate & Respond

Your personalized calm plan will appear here.

3 regulation steps

  • Take 6 slow breaths, longer on the exhale.
  • Unclench jaw and lower shoulders for 20 seconds.
  • Drink water and feel your feet on the floor.

Communication options

  • “I’m feeling overwhelmed. Can we pause?”
  • “I want to talk, but I need a calmer moment.”
  • “I’m not okay right now. I need support.”

Boundary options

  • “I’m stepping away for 10 minutes.”
  • “Please don’t raise your voice at me.”
  • “I’m available to talk when we can be respectful.”

I’m scared to talk mode

  • Text a trusted person one line: “Could you check in with me today?”
  • Ask for company without explaining everything: “Can you stay with me for a bit?”
  • Write your thoughts first, then share only what feels safe.
