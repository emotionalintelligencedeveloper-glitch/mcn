Domestic Violence: Safety & Gentle Support Check-In

You’re not alone. You deserve safety. If you’re hurting, scared to talk, or unsure how to explain what’s happening, this is a calm place to take one small step. You don’t have to explain everything today.

If you’re in immediate danger, call your local emergency number.

3) Create a Reach-Out Draft

You can choose a template, edit it, and copy it. No pressure.

4) Quiet Safety Planning (only what feels safe)

Only do what is safe in your situation.

Identify a safe contact
One person who is calm and trustworthy. You can keep contact details in a place only you can access.
Pack essentials list (only if safe)
If safe, think about: ID, keys, medications, essential documents, and emergency contacts.
Choose a code word with a trusted person
A neutral word or phrase can signal "I need help now" without details.
Consider device safety
If needed, clear browser history, use private mode, and sign out of shared devices.
